# timothygaull.com

Astro site deployed on Cloudflare Workers (`wrangler.jsonc`). GitHub Pages only serves the redirect in `github-pages-redirect/`.

## Writing or editing case studies

Before drafting or editing a case study in `src/pages/work/`, or any page meant to rank in search, read `docs/seo-strategy.md` and work through `docs/case-study-seo-checklist.md`. The demand and competition figures in the strategy are estimates, not search volumes. Prefer real Search Console data once it exists.
